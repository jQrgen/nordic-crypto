Good evening. This is Nordic Crypto. Tonight: how crypto and bitcoin rules are decided and enforced in Denmark.

[path @ ~18s, section - THE LEGAL PATH]
Sweden, Denmark and Finland are EU members, so MiCA applies directly as EU law.
MiCA applies directly as EU law. Finanstilsynet authorises crypto-asset service providers under MiCA Article 63, as set out in the Financial Business Act.

[actors @ ~40s, section - WHO DECIDES]
In Denmark, Erhvervsministeriet prepares the financial-markets bills. Folketinget adopts the national acts. The national law that shapes crypto supervision here is the Financial Business Act provisions on MiCA authorisation: Lov om finansiel virksomhed § 332 c.

[supervise @ ~65s, section - WHO SUPERVISES]
Finanstilsynet is the financial supervisor that licences and oversees crypto-asset service providers under MiCA. Danmarks Nationalbank is the central bank for monetary policy and payments; it is not the MiCA licensing authority.

[enforce @ ~90s, section - WHO ENFORCES]
Suspicious transactions are reported to the financial intelligence unit: Hvidvasksekretariatet within NSK. Tax on crypto-assets is handled by Skattestyrelsen. Disputes and appeals go to the ordinary courts, with administrative review of supervisory decisions where the law provides for it.

[status @ ~115s, section - WHERE THINGS STAND]
There is no separate crypto tax act; Skattestyrelsen applies the general tax rules to crypto-assets.

[signoff @ ~140s, end]
