Nordic Crypto #2: Data-centre halt proposals in the Storting and new fraud warnings in Sweden

Nordic Crypto has published issue #2 of its weekly newsletter on crypto, bitcoin and blockchain news from the Nordics, covering 5 October 2026 – 12 October 2026.

Key stories:
- The Red Party (Rødt) has proposed in the Storting a temporary halt for new data centres and a licensing regime.
  https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Representantforslag/2026-2027/dok8-202627-006s/
- Socialist Left Party (SV) MPs propose putting new data centres on hold and ask the government to report on the follow-up of the Storting's crypto-mining ban. This is a proposal, not a decision.
  https://stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Publikasjoner/Representantforslag/2026-2027/dok8-202627-009s
- Customers of the Norwegian exchange Firi bought NOK 16.1 million more crypto than they sold in September, according to Finansavisen.
  https://www.finansavisen.no/valuta/2026/10/05/8385368/smasparere-hamstret-krypto-under-renteuro
- Sweden's Finansinspektionen has warned against 30 more unlicensed investment firms, 227 so far in 2026.
  https://nyheter24.se/nyheter/inrikes/1519244-fi-varnar-for-fler-bluffaktorer
- The EU anti-money-laundering authority AMLA has sent three draft technical standards to the European Commission, including rules for crypto-asset service providers.
  https://www.fi.se/sv/publicerat/nyheter/2026/amla-foreslar-tre-tekniska-standarder-enligt-penningtvattsforordningen/
