Nordic Crypto #2: Data-centre halt proposals in the Storting and new fraud warnings in Sweden

Nordic Crypto has published issue #2 of its weekly newsletter on crypto, bitcoin and blockchain news from the Nordics, covering 5 October 2026 – 12 October 2026.

Key stories

  • The Red Party (Rødt) has proposed in the Storting a temporary halt for new data centres and a licensing regime. www.stortinget.no
  • Socialist Left Party (SV) MPs propose putting new data centres on hold and ask the government to report on the follow-up of the Storting's crypto-mining ban. This is a proposal, not a decision. stortinget.no
  • Customers of the Norwegian exchange Firi bought NOK 16.1 million more crypto than they sold in September, according to Finansavisen. www.finansavisen.no
  • Sweden's Finansinspektionen has warned against 30 more unlicensed investment firms, 227 so far in 2026. nyheter24.se
  • The EU anti-money-laundering authority AMLA has sent three draft technical standards to the European Commission, including rules for crypto-asset service providers. www.fi.se
