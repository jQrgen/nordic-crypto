Good evening. This is Nordic Crypto. Tonight: how crypto and bitcoin rules are decided and enforced in Sweden.

[path @ ~18s, section - THE LEGAL PATH]
Sweden, Denmark and Finland are EU members, so MiCA applies directly as EU law.
MiCA applies directly as EU law. Sweden's complementary act names Finansinspektionen as the national competent authority.

[actors @ ~40s, section - WHO DECIDES]
In Sweden, Finansdepartementet prepares the financial-markets bills. Riksdagen adopts the national acts. The national law that shapes crypto supervision here is the Swedish MiCA complementary act: Lag (2024:1159).

[supervise @ ~65s, section - WHO SUPERVISES]
Finansinspektionen is the financial supervisor that licences and oversees crypto-asset service providers under MiCA. Sveriges Riksbank works on the e-krona and payment systems; it is not the MiCA licensing authority.

[enforce @ ~90s, section - WHO ENFORCES]
Suspicious transactions are reported to the financial intelligence unit: Finanspolisen at Polismyndigheten. Tax on crypto-assets is handled by Skatteverket. Disputes and appeals go to the general courts (tingsrätt, hovrätt, Högsta domstolen) and administrative courts for licence appeals.

[status @ ~115s, section - WHERE THINGS STAND]
One FI-authorised CASP (Safello) as of 30 Jun 2026, plus 77 firms passporting from other EU countries. Two refusals (Valuno's QB Europe AB, GreenMerc's Ijort Invest AB). A new government was still being formed in early October 2026.
