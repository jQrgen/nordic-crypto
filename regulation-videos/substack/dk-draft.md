🇩🇰 **Denmark** — who makes and enforces the crypto rules?

This short explainer is from **Nordic Crypto**. It follows the same sourced map as our [How the rules are made](https://nordiccrypto.no/rules/) page.

### The legal path
MiCA applies directly as EU law. Finanstilsynet authorises crypto-asset service providers under MiCA Article 63, as set out in the Financial Business Act.

National act: **Lov om finansiel virksomhed § 332 c**.

### Who decides
- **Ministry:** Erhvervsministeriet prepares the bills.
- **Parliament:** Folketinget adopts the acts.

### Who supervises
- **Financial supervisor (MiCA competent authority):** Finanstilsynet
- **Central bank:** Danmarks Nationalbank — Danmarks Nationalbank is the central bank for monetary policy and payments; it is not the MiCA licensing authority.

### Who enforces
- **FIU:** Hvidvasksekretariatet within NSK
- **Tax:** Skattestyrelsen
- **Courts:** the ordinary courts, with administrative review of supervisory decisions where the law provides for it

### Status snapshot (Oct 2026)
There is no separate crypto tax act; Skattestyrelsen applies the general tax rules to crypto-assets.
