🇳🇴 **Norway** — who makes and enforces the crypto rules?

This short explainer is from **Nordic Crypto**. It follows the same sourced map as our [How the rules are made](https://nordiccrypto.no/rules/) page.

### The legal path
MiCA applies in Norway through the EEA Agreement, given effect by the Crypto-Assets Act from 1 July 2025.

National act: **Lov om kryptoeiendeler (LOV-2025-05-27-20)**.

### Who decides
- **Ministry:** Finansdepartementet prepares the bills.
- **Parliament:** Stortinget adopts the acts.

### Who supervises
- **Financial supervisor (MiCA competent authority):** Finanstilsynet
- **Central bank:** Norges Bank — Norges Bank oversees payment systems and studies central-bank digital currency; it is not the MiCA licensing authority.

### Who enforces
- **FIU:** Økokrim
- **Tax:** Skatteetaten
- **Courts:** the ordinary courts (tingrett, lagmannsrett, Høyesterett)

### Status snapshot (Oct 2026)
Six CASPs authorised by Finanstilsynet as of 3 Oct 2026: Týr Markets, Firi, K33 Markets, Bare Bitcoin, NBX and AK Jensen Norway. A temporary ban on new power-intensive crypto data centres is being studied but not adopted.
