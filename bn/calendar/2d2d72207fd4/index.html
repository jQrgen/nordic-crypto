The first CBDC conference was held in Bergen in October 2022, with Simula and the University of Bergen. The conference was research-oriented, with participants from academia, the FinTech community and central banks. Topics included Norges Bank's CBDC work, DeFi and legal issues, AML and CBDC, data protection, quantum technology and OpenCBDC. The conference was the kick-off for a hackathon on transferring CBDC tokens through bridges between openCBDC and EVM-compatible networks.
