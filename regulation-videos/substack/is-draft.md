🇮🇸 **Iceland** — who makes and enforces the crypto rules?

This short explainer is from **Nordic Crypto**. It follows the same sourced map as our [How the rules are made](https://nordiccrypto.no/rules/) page.

### The legal path
Iceland is outside the EU but in the EEA. The EEA Joint Committee incorporated MiCA; Alþingi gave it effect through Act No. 101/2025. From 1 July 2026 crypto-asset services require a licence.

National act: **Lög nr. 101/2025**.

### Who decides
- **Ministry:** Fjármála- og efnahagsráðuneytið prepares the bills.
- **Parliament:** Alþingi adopts the acts.

### Who supervises
- **Financial supervisor (MiCA competent authority):** Seðlabanki Íslands – Fjármálaeftirlit (Financial Supervision)
- **Central bank:** Seðlabanki Íslands — Unlike the other Nordics, the Central Bank of Iceland also houses financial supervision (Fjármálaeftirlit), so the same institution both issues monetary policy and licences crypto-asset service providers under MiCA.

### Who enforces
- **FIU:** Skrifstofa fjármálagreininga lögreglu
- **Tax:** Skatturinn
- **Courts:** the Icelandic courts, including Hæstiréttur for final appeal

### Status snapshot (Oct 2026)
One CASP authorised (Myntkaup, 25 Jun 2026) and one e-money token issuer (Monerium) in the ESMA register.
