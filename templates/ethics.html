Press ethics

Nordic Crypto follows Vær Varsom-plakaten, the ethical code of the Norwegian Press Association (Norsk Presseforbund).

The Press Association publishes the code at Vær Varsom-plakaten. This page is our own account of how we apply it on Nordic Crypto. It is not a copy of the code and not an official translation.

Following the code is a standard we set for this site. Nordic Crypto is not a member of the Norwegian Press Association, and the Press Complaints Commission (Pressens Faglige Utvalg) does not handle complaints about us.

What that means here

The publisher is Jørgen S. Notland (jQrgen), in Oslo. Human editors decide what is published. Nothing goes out on its own.

We link to the original report and write a short summary of our own. We do not copy article text, and we do not get around paywalls. Headlines and quotes stay as the source wrote them.

A news summary and a comment stay apart. A column is marked as a column.

We name our sources. The who's who and the academia page only say what those sources say. If we are unsure, we leave it out. People appear only in public professional roles.

We do not publish private information, health information or private financial information.

When we find a mistake, we correct it as soon as we can and say what changed. See corrections and removal. If you want an entry or a photo taken down, we take it down.

Nothing on Nordic Crypto is investment advice.

Read the code at the Norwegian Press Association. Questions about this site go through About Nordic Crypto.
