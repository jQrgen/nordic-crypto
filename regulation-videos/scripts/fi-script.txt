Good evening. This is Nordic Crypto. Tonight: how crypto and bitcoin rules are decided and enforced in Finland.

[path @ ~18s, section - THE LEGAL PATH]
Sweden, Denmark and Finland are EU members, so MiCA applies directly as EU law.
MiCA applies directly as EU law. Act 402/2024 supplements MiCA; under its section 2, Finanssivalvonta supervises compliance.

[actors @ ~40s, section - WHO DECIDES]
In Finland, Valtiovarainministeriö prepares the financial-markets bills. Eduskunta adopts the national acts. The national law that shapes crypto supervision here is Act 402/2024 on crypto-asset service providers and crypto-asset markets: Laki 402/2024.

[supervise @ ~65s, section - WHO SUPERVISES]
Finanssivalvonta (FIN-FSA) is the financial supervisor that licences and oversees crypto-asset service providers under MiCA. Suomen Pankki is the central bank; MiCA licensing and ongoing supervision of crypto-asset service providers sit with Finanssivalvonta.

[enforce @ ~90s, section - WHO ENFORCES]
Suspicious transactions are reported to the financial intelligence unit: Rahanpesun selvittelykeskus within the National Bureau of Investigation. Tax on crypto-assets is handled by Verohallinto. Disputes and appeals go to the general courts and administrative courts for appeals against supervisory decisions.

[status @ ~115s, section - WHERE THINGS STAND]
Five FIN-FSA-authorised CASPs in the ESMA register (Coinmotion, Tesseract, Bittimaatti, Northcrypto, Kvarn X) and one e-money token issuer (Paxos Issuance Europe). Some Swedish firms now operate on Finnish licences (Kvarn, Northcrypto).
