🇸🇪 **Sweden** — who makes and enforces the crypto rules?

This short explainer is from **Nordic Crypto**. It follows the same sourced map as our [How the rules are made](https://nordiccrypto.no/rules/) page.

### The legal path
MiCA applies directly as EU law. Sweden's complementary act names Finansinspektionen as the national competent authority.

National act: **Lag (2024:1159)**.

### Who decides
- **Ministry:** Finansdepartementet prepares the bills.
- **Parliament:** Riksdagen adopts the acts.

### Who supervises
- **Financial supervisor (MiCA competent authority):** Finansinspektionen
- **Central bank:** Sveriges Riksbank — Sveriges Riksbank works on the e-krona and payment systems; it is not the MiCA licensing authority.

### Who enforces
- **FIU:** Finanspolisen at Polismyndigheten
- **Tax:** Skatteverket
- **Courts:** the general courts (tingsrätt, hovrätt, Högsta domstolen) and administrative courts for licence appeals

### Status snapshot (Oct 2026)
One FI-authorised CASP (Safello) as of 30 Jun 2026, plus 77 firms passporting from other EU countries. Two refusals (Valuno's QB Europe AB, GreenMerc's Ijort Invest AB). A new government was still being formed in early October 2026.
