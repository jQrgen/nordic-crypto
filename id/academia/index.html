Akademisi: blockchain dan kripto di universitas Nordik

Mata kuliah dan program, perkumpulan mahasiswa, kelompok riset, dan publikasi tentang blockchain, bitcoin, dan kripto di Norwegia, Swedia, Denmark, Finlandia, dan Islandia. Setiap baris menautkan ke sumbernya dan menampilkan kapan terakhir kali kami memeriksanya.

Kelompok dan proyek riset

  1. Blockchain-UiO

    University of Oslo, Dept. of Informatics

    Academic-industrial blockchain lab at UiO's Department of Informatics. According to its own pages, it aims to facilitate blockchain research at UiO, improve education in blockchain technology through courses, tutorials and master projects, and promote collaboration between academia and industry. The newest dated item on its website (blockchainlab.no) is an open day on 1 December 2021.

    Sumber: mn.uio.no · diperiksa 2026-10-05

  2. Yggdrasil: Network Security for Blockchain and Decentralized Systems

    Chalmers University of Technology

    Research project (2026–) on network-layer security of blockchains, cross-chain bridges and peer-to-peer networks.

    Sumber: research.chalmers.se · diperiksa 2026-10-05

  3. RU FINTECH

    Reykjavík University

    FinTech incl. blockchain, crypto, regulation

    Sumber: ru.is · diperiksa 2026-10-03
