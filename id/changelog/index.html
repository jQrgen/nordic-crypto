Changelog

Changes to the Nordic Crypto site itself – new pages, sections and features – newest first. Daily news is not listed here.

  1. Aggregated prices per pair

    5 October 2026

    The markets API now has one aggregated price per base-quote pair at /api/v1/markets/aggregated.json, also on /api/v1/markets.json and /api/v1/markets/by-asset/{symbol}.json. BTC-NOK is not mixed with BTC-EUR. The figure is the mean of published last prices, or the mean of bid and ask when no last exists. Min, max, exchange count and the contributing prices are included. Volume is shown and summed only when an exchange published it, and a 24-hour volume is not added to an unlabeled one. There is no volume-weighted price. Coin icons from cryptocurrency-icons (CC0 1.0) are served under /api/v1/markets/logos/ when that set includes the asset. The markets page shows the aggregate and the volume.

  2. iOS app on TestFlight

    5 October 2026

    A public TestFlight invite for the Nordic Crypto iOS app is linked from the footer, the homepage, the markets page and About. There is no App Store listing.

  3. Prices on Nordic exchanges

    5 October 2026

    Market prices from Firi, Norwegian Block Exchange and Coinmotion are on /markets/ and in /api/v1/markets.json. Quotes are NOK, SEK, DKK and EUR. The figures are market data, not investment advice. Safello, Goobit, Trijo, Northcrypto, Kvarn X and any Danish- or Icelandic-registered venue without a public ticker are listed as skipped. The JSON is fetched at build time and refreshed on gh-pages about hourly.

  4. Editorial ethics

    5 October 2026

    Editorial ethics says Nordic Crypto follows Vær Varsom-plakaten. A named editor approves everything. The complaint address on the page is TBA. The page is at /ethics/, linked from About and the footer, in every site language.

  5. Public data API

    5 October 2026

    News, newsletters, the calendar, sources, academia, the who's who, profiles and the rules map are published as JSON under /api/v1/. No account is required. The guide is at /api/, with OpenAPI and llms.txt for other tools.

  6. Issue #1 newsreel re-recorded

    5 October 2026

    Re-recorded the spoken track of newsletter issue #1 (release newsletter-001-newsreel-3) so it matches the captions. The opening does not say the programme is made with artificial intelligence. Kaupr is named only as the source of the GreenMerc story. The sign-off is the Nordic Crypto team.

  7. Issue #1 newsreel live

    5 October 2026

    Replaced the live newsletter issue #1 video with the corrected newsreel (release newsletter-001-newsreel-2). Kaupr is named only when a story comes from Kaupr. The issue no longer opens by saying it is made with artificial intelligence.

  8. Nordic Crypto on the map, in the newsletter, and for columnists

    5 October 2026

    The public name is Nordic Crypto. EBA, ESMA and the EBA chair are shown as EU on the industry map. Newsletter issue #1 can be read in English, Norwegian (nynorsk and bokmål), Swedish, Danish, Finnish and Icelandic. When a story is in another language, the card explains it in the language of the page. Apply as a columnist from the footer, About and the newsletter. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits in aggregate, without cookies, and that data is not sold. Summaries are made with the help of artificial intelligence, with human editors (jQrgen and the Nordic Crypto editor).

  9. Newsletter tab and the first issue, with video

    5 October 2026

    The main menu has a new Newsletter tab. It lists every issue, newest first, next to the Substack signup. Issue #1 (Binance under scrutiny and Sweden's crypto sanctions warning) can be read in full on the site, with a short video that plays from this website, with English subtitles and no third-party player.

  10. The name is Nordic Crypto

    5 October 2026

    The site is called Nordic Crypto. The newsletter signup links to https://cryptonordic.substack.com/subscribe. The domain cryptonordic.no and the X account @xcryptonordic stay as they are.

  11. Send us a tip

    3 October 2026

    Readers can now tip Nordic Crypto about articles via the new Send a tip page, which opens a form on GitHub. Tips are public on GitHub, so please don't include personal or sensitive information. Our editor checks every tip against our rules; a tip does not guarantee publication.

  12. Past events archive

    3 October 2026

    Finished events are no longer removed from the calendar. Once an event has ended (Oslo time) it moves automatically to a new Past events section at the bottom of the calendar, newest first. The office screen shows only upcoming events.

  13. Denmark added

    3 October 2026

    Nordic Crypto now covers Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Iceland. Denmark has a country filter on the news, calendar, who's who and academia pages, and we now follow Danish newspapers, broadcasters, tech media, Danmarks Nationalbank, Kaupr's Denmark page and a news search on Danish sites. Danish stories, events and who's who entries appear once our editor has reviewed them.

  14. Coverage narrowed to four countries

    3 October 2026

    Nordic Crypto now covers Norway, Sweden, Finland and Iceland. The Faroe Islands have been removed from the news sources, country filters, calendar, who's who, Regulation by country section, academia page and office screen.

  15. Academia page

    3 October 2026

    New Academia page: blockchain and crypto courses and programmes, student associations, publications and research groups at universities in Norway, Sweden, Finland, Iceland and the Faroe Islands. Every row shows its source, the date we checked it and its status; only editor-approved rows are shown.

  16. Nordic Crypto launches

    3 October 2026

    First version of the site in English: news from Norway, Sweden, Finland, Iceland and the Faroe Islands with our own short summaries; an events calendar with a month grid, a mobile list and a country filter; a who's who of the industry and its regulators split into private and public sector, with a country filter; a Regulation by country section; the list of sources we follow; an About page with corrections and removal; and a full-screen office screen mode for portrait and landscape displays.
